About me

I am a PhD student in Mathematics at the Institute of Mathematics of the University of Seville (IMUS). My thesis, Fluid Interfaces: Well-Posedness, Regularity and Instabilities, is supervised by Francisco Gancedo and Eduardo García-Juárez. Prior to that, I completed my Master’s degree at the Complutense University of Madrid (UCM) and my Bachelor’s degree at the University of Seville.

My research is in the analysis of partial differential equations arising in fluid mechanics. I am particularly interested in free boundary problems for incompressible fluids, such as the moving interface between two fluids and the instabilities that can develop along it. More broadly, I enjoy exploring new problems in PDEs and fluid dynamics, and I am always open to learning from neighbouring areas.

Beyond research, I enjoy sharing mathematics with young students through talks and workshops in schools, with a particular interest in encouraging girls to pursue science.
